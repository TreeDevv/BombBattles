# Bomb Battles Game Brief

## Game Overview

**Bomb Battles** is a fast-paced Roblox team arena game inspired by classic Minecraft-style Bomb Lobbers. Players spawn on separate floating islands and throw bombs across the gap, trying to eliminate the opposing team before their own team is wiped out.

The game is meant to be instantly understandable: pick a team, spawn on an island, throw bombs, dodge explosions, survive the round.

## Core Fantasy

Players are chaotic artillery fighters in a bright, readable island arena. Every throw should feel exciting: bombs arc through the air, land with clear danger, explode with satisfying impact, and force players to move, aim, and react under pressure.

The fantasy is not about complex loadouts or long-term strategy at first. It is about simple team chaos, close calls, clutch throws, and the thrill of watching the other island fall apart one elimination at a time.

## Design Pillars

- **Easy to understand:** Players should know what to do within seconds.
- **Quick rounds:** Matches should move fast, reset cleanly, and invite immediate replays.
- **Team tension:** Players win or lose together, creating shared moments and comeback potential.
- **Satisfying explosions:** Bombs should feel powerful, readable, and fun to use.
- **Readable danger:** Players should be able to understand where bombs are landing and how to avoid them.
- **Playful chaos:** The game should feel energetic and silly without becoming impossible to read.

## Core Gameplay Loop

1. Players enter a lobby or intermission.
2. Players are assigned to teams.
3. Each team spawns on its own island.
4. Players receive or collect bombs.
5. Players throw bombs at the opposing island and team.
6. Players dodge incoming bombs and avoid being eliminated.
7. A team wins when the other team has no surviving players.
8. The round resets and the loop begins again.

## Match Flow

- **Intermission:** Players gather, wait for the next match, and prepare for team assignment.
- **Team assignment:** Players are split into opposing teams.
- **Island spawn:** Teams spawn on separate islands with enough space to move, aim, and dodge.
- **Active battle:** Players throw bombs, avoid explosions, and try to survive.
- **Elimination:** Players who die are removed from the active round.
- **Win state:** The last team with surviving players wins.
- **Reset:** The arena resets quickly so the next match can start.

## Player Objective

The player's objective is to help their team eliminate the opposing team by throwing bombs accurately, dodging enemy bombs, staying alive, and creating pressure on the enemy island.

The team objective is simple: be the last team standing.

## MVP Scope

The first playable version should focus on the smallest complete version of Bomb Battles:

- One arena with two opposing islands.
- Two teams.
- Basic bomb throwing.
- Bomb arc, landing, explosion, damage, and knockback.
- Player death and round elimination.
- Round start, win condition, and reset.
- Simple UI for round state, team status, and winner announcement.

This MVP should prove that the core game is fun before adding extra systems.

## Future Expansion Ideas

- More bomb types with different behaviors.
- Destructible or changing islands.
- Powerups that appear during rounds.
- Multiple arenas with different layouts.
- Round modifiers that change how each match plays.
- Cosmetics for bombs, trails, victory effects, and player expression.
- Progression tied to playtime, wins, or challenges.
- Spectating for eliminated players.
- Casual and competitive queue variants.

## Current Direction

Bomb Battles should start as a simple, readable, team-based island combat game. The first goal is to make throwing bombs, dodging explosions, and winning quick rounds feel good. Everything else should build on that foundation.
