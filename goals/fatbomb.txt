Fat Bomb — Flare Summon Flow

Total time from flare landing to damage: about 0.9–1.1 seconds.

1. Throw the flare

The player throws a small glowing flare using the same readable arc as a normal bomb.

While airborne, it should have:

A bright red-orange trail
A faint smoke ribbon
A rising flare whistle
A unique projectile silhouette so enemies recognize the ability immediately
2. Flare lands and activates

When it hits the ground, it sticks upright or embeds at a slight angle.

Immediately:

It spits sparks upward.
A thin column of colored smoke shoots into the sky.
The full damage radius appears around it.
A circular shadow appears beneath the flare.
A loud flare pop confirms the target location.

The warning circle should appear at full size instantly rather than slowly expanding.

3. The incoming giant is revealed

About 0.2 seconds after the flare lands, the camera-facing smoke column parts near the top and the giant Roblox character appears directly above it.

He should already be falling quickly rather than spawning extremely high up.

Visual pose:

Sitting or belly-first
Arms and legs flailing
Slight body wobble
Clothes fluttering upward
A few downward speed streaks
Slowly rotating off-center

The smoke column visually connects the flare to the falling character, making it feel like the flare called him in.

4. Final warning

As he descends:

The ground shadow rapidly expands.
The flare sparks faster.
The danger circle pulses twice.
Wind streaks spiral inward toward the center.
The falling whistle becomes deeper and louder.

During the final 0.1 seconds, the flare extinguishes and the whistle abruptly cuts out.

No gameplay freeze or slow motion is needed.

5. Impact

The giant lands directly on the flare.

At contact:

His body squashes outward slightly.
The flare disappears beneath him.
A sharp circular shockwave crosses the damage radius.
Dust and chunky debris burst outward.
Nearby players are launched away from the center.
A shallow circular crater decal appears underneath him.

Damage should happen immediately on contact, synchronized with the first shockwave.

6. Fast exit

He should only remain for around 0.3 seconds after impact.

A clean exit could be:

He bounces once from the force of landing.
A smoke cloud briefly covers him.
He disappears inside the smoke.
The smoke collapses into a few fading flare sparks.

This is faster and less visually obstructive than having a hook slowly pull him back into the sky.

Final rhythm

Throw flare → flare lands → smoke beacon activates → giant appears overhead → shadow expands → impact → smoke-pop disappearance

The flare remains the visual anchor throughout the sequence, so players instantly understand where the attack will land without needing excessive UI or a long cinematic.
